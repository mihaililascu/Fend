A payment link lets housemates pay you your share in one tap — straight into your Revolut or PayPal. Fend never sees the money; it just adds your link to the message you send.

📲 Revolut

Go to paypal.me, sign in, and claim your link. It looks like paypal.me/yourname. Copy and paste it below.
