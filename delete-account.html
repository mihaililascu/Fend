Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your Fend account and everything associated with it at any time. Here's how.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@getfendapp.com from the email address you use to sign in to Fend.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my account.
  3. We'll confirm your request and permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account — the email address and login you created.
  • All data you added in Fend — your bills and subscriptions, including their amounts, dates, billing cycles, reference numbers and any notes.

What we keep

Nothing. Once your account is deleted, none of your personal data or bill information is retained by Fend.

Your country and currency preference is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us — it is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.
